WELCOME TO THE CLUB

Aim. Swing. Outplay.

  1. Xbox controller: in Edge on Xbox, open this site and press and release a controller button. If the controller moves the browser cursor, hold Menu to switch to game controls. Use the left stick to aim, hold LT for fine adjustment, and hold A or RT for power. Release, then press A or RT at the centre mark. B cancels, X toggles jumping, and Y faces the next wicket. The right stick looks around and zooms; LB/RB change views, or choose between your two balls when offered. During placement, left/right positions your ball and A confirms. Menu opens settings; View opens Help. Use D-pad up/down to navigate menus, left/right to change settings, A to select and B to return. The right stick scrolls Help.
  2. Start: two-player games start with a coin toss. In Golf and Backyard, the winner chooses colours and Blue starts. In Association, the winner chooses the lead or colours; the other side gets the remaining choice. Three- and four-player club games draw colours by lot. Your scoreboard shows your assigned colours.
  3. Aim: tap or click the lawn. The dotted line shows your chosen direction, with yard marks along the lawn. Each shot starts aimed toward your next wicket (or stake); click to change direction and fine-tune it yourself. In Shot view, the camera stays fixed while you aim; tap Shot view again to recenter. Fine-tune with the slider or ← → keys. Hold the middle mouse button over the lawn and drag to look around. Release it to return to your previous view.
  4. Swing: hold the left mouse button in the highlighted START HERE zone on the right of the swing pad and pull left to set power. Full power is reachable within the pad. On touchscreens, pull down. Pull farther for a stronger shot; ease back to reduce power. Hold Shift on desktop for fine power adjustment. Release to lock power and start the forward swing, then click or tap anywhere in the game as the mallet reaches the ball. The timing marker stays where you tapped so you can see whether you were early or late. This click is captured even if you released over Fine Aim or outside the pad. Early or late timing adds a gentle directional error; thumb wobble does not affect accuracy. Releasing without pulling cancels. For keyboard play, hold Space to build power, release, then press Space again as the mallet reaches the ball. Escape cancels. If you miss the accuracy window, the shot strikes at the far end. Yard marks and estimated roll help judge distance. Select Roll shot to try a Jump shot; Power controls height and carry. Jump shots have more variation in loft and direction, and mistimed contact can produce a lower jump. The computer may jump over a blocker.
  5. Association placement: after a roquet, the mallet disappears and the view closes in overhead. Tap or drag on the side of the other ball where you want to place your ball, or adjust the position slider. Your ball stays in contact. The colored list shows each ball’s next hoop or stake, with north/south direction; matching color tags appear at its wicket in placement views. Use Court or Overhead to see the other wickets, or Close to return. In Close view, Look around rotates the camera and Zoom out widens your view without moving either ball. The scroll wheel also zooms during placement. Confirm placement to bring back the mallet and open Shot view aimed toward your next wicket. Adjust the aim for the croquet stroke as needed. Use Reposition ball to change sides before swinging.
  6. Score: send the whole ball through its highlighted hoop in the arrow direction, or hit the highlighted stake when it is next in your sequence.
  7. Keep your bearings: the clock-tower pavilion always marks the north end of the lawn.
  8. Computer level: choose Amateur, Country Club Champ, Pro, or Master from the top menu. Pro keeps the original computer play; Master is a little more accurate. The easier levels have less precise direction and distance control. Your shot accuracy is a separate option in Settings. Amateur gives you steadier shots. Pro adds more directional variation, especially at higher power. Center your strike in either mode for the most accurate shot.
  9. Play tactically: knock opponents away, block a hoop, or set up your second ball. A knocked-through ball scores for its owner.

Game rules

Association and Backyard support 2, 3, or 4 players with one human. Two players use the usual two-ball sides. With 3 or 4 players, this app uses a club free-for-all: one ball each, colours drawn by lot, then Blue, Red, Black, and Yellow order. First player to finish their ball’s course wins; bonus-shot rules still apply. This is a custom multiplayer format.

Controller & game settings

Press and release a controller button to connect.

Left stick: aim or place ball · LT: fine control
A or RT: hold for power, release, then press again at centre
Right stick: look around and zoom · B: cancel
X: roll/jump · Y: face next wicket
LB/RB: camera views (choose ball when offered)
Menu: settings · View: Help
Menus: D-pad or left stick to move, ← → to change, A to select, B to return. Right stick scrolls Help.

Changing the game, player count, or opposition starts a new game and clears the score. On Xbox, open this site in Edge. If the controller moves a browser cursor, hold Menu to switch to game controls.
